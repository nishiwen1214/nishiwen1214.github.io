01 / Profile

个人简介 / About

中文简介

我现任深圳理工大学人工智能研究院副教授。此前曾在先进计算与数字工程研究所、中国科学院深圳先进技术研究院担任助理研究员、博士后。我于国立成功大学计算机科学与信息工程系获得博士学位。2023 年获选斐陶斐荣誉学会荣誉会员，获中国台湾信息学会最佳博士论文奖“杰出论文奖”，并入选深圳先进院院长优秀博士后专项计划；2024 年入选国家级人才计划；2025 年获评“深圳市优秀博士后”及深圳先进院“十大优秀博士后”。研究方向包括深度学习、自然语言处理、大语言模型与 AI for Science。

English Profile

I am an Associate Professor at the Artificial Intelligence Research Institute, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. I was previously an Assistant Professor/Postdoc at the Institute of Advanced Computing and Digital Engineering, Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, National Cheng Kung University. In 2023, I was selected as an honorary member of the Phi Tau Phi Scholastic Honor Society, received the Outstanding Dissertation Award of the Taiwan Institute of Information & Computing Machinery, and joined SIAT's Dean's Postdoctoral Merit Special Programme. In 2024, I was selected for a National Level Talent Programme. In 2025, I received the Shenzhen Excellent Postdoctoral Fellow award and was named one of SIAT's Top 10 Outstanding Postdoctoral Researchers. My research interests include deep learning, natural language processing, large language models, and AI for Science.
